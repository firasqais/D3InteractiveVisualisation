SVG anatomy: what D3 actually draws

D3 does not paint pixels. It creates and edits SVG elements, which are plain text that describe shapes. This chart is hand-written SVG with no D3 at all. Click any part of it to see its markup.

What to notice

  • The origin is top-left and y grows downward. A taller bar needs a smaller y. Dubai is 186 px tall and the baseline is at y = 200, so its y is 200 − 186 = 14.
  • Every bar is a <rect> with x, y, width and height. Labels are <text>; the baseline is a <line>.
  • The <g transform="translate(50,20)"> shifts the whole plot. That is the margin convention you'll meet in example 05.
